\begin{abstract}
We introduce sparse mixture adapters (SMA) for low-resource translation.
SMA reaches 29.1 BLEU on WMT14 English--German, 1.8 BLEU above the Transformer baseline,
while training 4.2$\times$ faster.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Attention-based models \citep{vaswani2017attention} and pre-trained encoders \citep{devlin2019bert}
dominate translation. Adapters \citep{houlsby2019parameter} make fine-tuning cheap, and recursive
gradient folding \citep{li2023recursive} has been proposed for the same purpose.
Results are in Table~\ref{tab:main}; the ablation is in Table~\ref{tab:ablaton}.

\section{Results}
SMA improves BLEU from 27.3 to 28.9, a 9.5\% relative gain, and cuts training time from 42 to 10 hours,
a 4.2$\times$ speedup.
